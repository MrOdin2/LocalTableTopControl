Overview

The Canvas plugin gives the DM a freeform picture board for sharing images with players. Pictures can be loaded from the file system, freely positioned, resized, and rotated in the DM panel. The DM then chooses which pictures are projected onto the player-facing table view as a transparent overlay on top of the active map.

Use the Canvas to share handouts, illustrations, portraits, props, clue images, or any other visual aid without leaving the session.

DM View

The Canvas panel shows a dashed-border workspace that represents the player-facing table view. The toolbar at the top provides quick access to common actions.

Toolbar controlDescription
Share All toggleInstantly shares (or un-shares) every picture on the canvas with the player table.
Add picture… buttonOpens a file-chooser dialog to load an image from disk.

Right-clicking on an empty area of the workspace also opens a mini context menu with a Load picture… option. The picture is placed at the right-click position.

Loading Pictures

Use either method to add a picture:

  • Click Add picture… in the toolbar.
  • Right-click on the workspace canvas and choose Load picture….

Supported formats: PNG, JPG/JPEG, GIF, BMP, WEBP. The file-chooser remembers the last directory used.

New pictures are placed with a default size (approximately 30 % of the canvas width). You can resize and reposition them immediately after loading.

Arranging Pictures

Click a picture to select it. Selection reveals the resize corners and rotate handles.

InteractionHow to perform it
Move Click and drag anywhere on the picture (not on a handle).
Resize Drag one of the four corner handles (small squares at the corners). The picture scales from the dragged corner. Works correctly even when the picture is rotated.
Rotate Drag one of the four edge handles (small circles at the mid-point of each edge). The picture rotates around its centre.
Deselect Click on an empty area of the workspace.
Remove Right-click the picture and choose Remove.

Sharing with Players

Each picture has an independent sharing toggle:

  • Right-click the picture and tick Share with Table to project it.
  • Un-tick to hide it from players without removing it from the canvas.

The Share All toggle button in the toolbar flips the sharing state of every picture at once — handy for a quick reveal or blackout.

Tip: You can pre-load all pictures for a session, arrange them, and then share them one by one as the narrative unfolds. Players only see the pictures you explicitly share.

Table View Overlay

Shared pictures appear as a transparent overlay rendered on top of the active primary table view (Map or DynamicMap). There is no interference with the map — the canvas overlay is fully mouse-transparent on the player screen.

Picture positions and sizes in the DM workspace correspond directly to the same relative positions on the player table: a picture placed in the top-left quarter of the DM workspace appears in the top-left quarter of the player screen, scaled to the actual screen resolution.

The overlay is visible only when at least one picture has sharing enabled. If no pictures are shared, the overlay is completely invisible.

Tips & Best Practices

  • Use the Share All toggle to quickly hide all pictures when transitioning between scenes.
  • Load and arrange all handout images before the session to avoid scrambling during play.
  • To show a full-screen illustration, resize it to cover the entire workspace area, then share it. It will fill the player screen on top of the map.
  • Shared pictures layer in the order they were added — the most recently added picture appears on top.
